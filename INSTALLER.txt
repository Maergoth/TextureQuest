TextureQuest 1.9.2 — Windows x64

1. If you used Replace PAK mode, restore stock files with EQ2's patcher first.
2. Close EQ2. Extract this app outside the game folder; keep data beside the EXE.
3. Choose your game folder, then Install graphics hook. Setup is automatic.
4. Launch EQ2 normally, including after reboot. TextureQuest can stay closed.

Numpad * switches Original/Upscaled. Use Shortcut to change it.
Home opens ReShade. Its other configurable shortcuts are disabled. Settings →
Reset ReShade shortcuts reapplies this while keeping your TextureQuest shortcut.

About 17.7 GB downloads: our generated/corrected channels and lookup metadata.
The app builds about 27.8 GB of local textures using your own game files for
protected original channels, DDS headers and tails. Those parts are not downloaded.
No game PAK writes, manual mapping, extra tools or background watcher. Keep data.
Verified cached work is reused; interrupted downloads resume safely.

The hook uses stock full-add-on ReShade. Existing compatible ReShade is reused;
otherwise official ReShade is added as dxgi.dll. Unknown proxies stay untouched.
Close EQ2 before installing, uninstalling or activating updates. Uninstall removes
only owned, unchanged hook files and keeps the local texture cache.

After patches, unchanged assets still match. Verify / Update textures refreshes
the map; changed or unavailable sources stay original with a coverage report.
Get app update downloads, verifies, replaces and restarts the app, preserving data.

Includes third-party notices. Graphics hooks are not officially approved;
account-policy risk is unknown. The unfinished generation batch is not included.
