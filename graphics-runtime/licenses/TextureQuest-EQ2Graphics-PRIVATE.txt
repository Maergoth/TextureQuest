TextureQuest private adapter: managed default-off ABI, deferred master transitions
after frame cleanup, original sampler descriptor retention/restoration, D3D11
device ownership guards, and the upstream controls embedded in TextureQuest Home.
Compact controls and a one-time backed-up numeric preset migration were added.
AF uses16x; native lamp lighting is the default, with rebuilt lighting Advanced.
Character softness defaults to0.15. One version2 migration moves the former
0.05 default to0.15, backing it up and retaining other custom values andv1history.
The preserving path leaves native lamp shaders and fade coordinates unchanged.
Feature choices and later Advanced edits are retained. No game PAKs are modified.
Particle-emissive admission and nearby-light appearance are experimental and
require the actual game test; unsupported sources remain native. Very small
particles may be missed by the bounded radiance measurement.
Upstream offscreen lamps and smooth indoor transitions are included.
Projected window lights retain their own direction; native lamp rebuilding stays
off by default. Native lamps exclude their proven bounded sibling fixture submeshes
from their shadow maps; nearby unrelated objects remain shadow casters.

Renderer009 adds an aggregate selected-world-feature gate. Point shadows, AO
and the visible Sun lighting umbrella control world light/depth processing;
AF/text filtering do not wake scene work. All world choices off bypass hot scene
callbacks before device/native queries. One boundary cleanup clears old scene
state; re-enable rediscovers fresh targets/camera while retaining preferences.
Sun lighting off also gates saved direction/indoor/softness settings. Explicit
frame capture can temporarily request diagnostic tracking without enabling effects.
Filtering changes restore already-bound sampler substitutions.

Compilation and CPU review are not proof of EQ2 visuals or performance.
