TextureQuest uses a narrow subset of NVAPI public interface declarations from
NVIDIA/nvapi commit 70d337db9186e968eab622f7e786de7e437faf3d (SDK 61683).
The installed NVIDIA driver supplies nvapi64.dll. No driver/proxy DLL is bundled.
Successful initialization and the module reference stay resident until process
exit. This prevents deinitializing NVAPI before the native device's final release,
which follows ReShade's addon/device callbacks. Turning the feature off removes
our device request, stops polling and keeps the initialized library. One reference
may remain per addon load; no native device is kept alive by an extra COM reference.
